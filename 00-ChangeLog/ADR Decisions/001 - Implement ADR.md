# ADR-001: Untitled 1

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
> What problem or situation is driving this decision?

Forgetting decisions I made and why, wanting to go back and review a timeline of changes I made to get to the spots I am in. 

## Decision
> What was decided?

We will implement a very simple form of ADR, not full on change control or documentation. But a light template that can track decisions made. 

## Consequences
> What does this change or affect going forward?

Pros: 
- Better change and decision documentation
- Full timeline of large changes and decisions
  
Cons: 
- More documentation work, less fun stuff
